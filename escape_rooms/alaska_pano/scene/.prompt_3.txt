This is a panorama. A 360-degree view from the centre of a dim planning room in a Hawaiian water-resources office at night. Ahead, a huge illuminated wall map of the Hawaiian islands, wells marked as small glowing pins, a few clustered and circled along one stretch of coastline. Below it a broad table with an open laptop showing the final well-chemistry plot, a two-way radio, and a coffee mug. Around: shelves of archived sample bottles, a corkboard of pinned field photos, and a wide window onto a volcanic coast under a starry sky. Warm amber lamplight against cool teal night, dramatic focused light on the map, muted painterly palette, subtle film grain. No people, no lettering, no captions, no UI text.
